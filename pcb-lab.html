    Learn how a numerical protection relay is built and how to diagnose it board by board. The lessons will cover each board's purpose, signal path, common faults, multimeter checks, when an oscilloscope is needed and what to report to senior engineers.

    • Measurement path
      1. CT / VT inputs
      2. Input conditioning
      3. ADC

    • Power path
      1. Auxiliary supply
      2. Converter
      3. DC rails
      4. Processor and circuits
    • Digital and communication paths
      1. Digital input
      2. Isolation
      3. Processor
      4. Driver
      5. Output relay
      1. Communication interface
      2. Processor

    These are illustrative architectures for learning. They are not the design of any specific manufacturer's relay.

    Safety: This is educational content. Company procedures and manufacturer instructions override any example here. Never open the secondary circuit of an energised CT, never probe live mains or SMPS primary circuits, and do not work on equipment you are not authorised and trained to work on.
